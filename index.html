Victor Lemos

Data Scientist & AI Engineer

I am a Data Scientist Specialist at Globo and an AI researcher with a Master’s degree in Computer Science from Universidade Federal do Maranhão (UFMA). My work centers on designing and deploying machine learning architectures across media, health tech, and enterprise systems.

At Globo, I build generative AI frameworks, including multi-agent systems for advertising personas, and develop first-party data models to predict household structures using statistical survey analysis. Previously, as a Senior Data Scientist consultant, I engineered advertising campaign simulators using PySpark and LightGBM, alongside automated feature pipelines on Google Cloud Platform.

My background combines production engineering with research in Computer Vision and NLP. As Lead Researcher at VIPLab, I developed automated diagnostic report generation systems for retinography images using Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and managed large-scale medical imaging datasets. Earlier in my career, I led machine learning projects focused on NLP trademark matching, BERT text classification, and phonetic scoring models, alongside published research on time-series forecasting using Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCN).

Across both industry and academic labs, my focus is on taking complex datasets, from medical images and natural language to broad consumer telemetry, and translating them into measurable, reliable production models.
